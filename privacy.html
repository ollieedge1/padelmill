Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

Who we are

Padel Mill Cambridge ("we") is developing Cambridge's first dedicated indoor padel club. Contact: hello@padelmill.co.uk.

What we collect

When you join our waitlist we collect your email address, your name (optional), and how often you'd like to play. Our forms are provided by Tally (tally.so), where this data is stored securely.

How we use it

We use your details to send occasional updates about the club — opening news, founder rates, events and early-access offers. We rely on your consent, given when you submit the form. We never sell your data or share it with third parties for their marketing.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics with IP anonymisation to understand how visitors use this site. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist details until the club opens and you become a member, or until you ask us to remove you — whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or to withdraw consent. Email hello@padelmill.co.uk and we'll action it promptly. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).
